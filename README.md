# Cat-Treats
Cat Treats Market Entry Analysis | 猫零食市场进入分析

Project Overview | 项目简介

This project uses Python to analyse the cat treats market, focusing on sales trends, competitive dynamics, pricing, and distribution channels. The goal is to identify market opportunities and develop data-driven market entry recommendations.

本项目使用 Python 对猫零食市场进行数据分析，重点研究销售趋势、竞争格局、价格变化和销售渠道表现，识别潜在市场机会，并提出数据驱动的市场进入策略。

Tools & Technologies | 使用工具

Python: Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib

Jupyter Notebook: Data cleaning and exploratory data analysis

PowerPoint: Data visualisation and business reporting

Key Analysis | 核心分析

Market Trends | 市场趋势： Sales value, volume, MoM and YoY growth / 销售额、销量及增长趋势

Competitive Analysis | 竞争分析： Product rankings and market share / 产品排名及市场份额

Pricing Analysis | 价格分析： Average selling price (ASP) / 平均售价及价格变化

Channel Analysis | 渠道分析： Sales performance across channels / 不同渠道的销售表现

Market Entry Strategy | 市场进入策略： Product positioning and growth opportunities / 产品定位及增长机会

Project Objective | 项目目标

Transform raw sales data into actionable business insights to support market entry and strategic decision-making.

将原始销售数据转化为可执行的商业洞察，为市场进入和战略决策提供支持。
